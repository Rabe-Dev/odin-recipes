Easy Crispy Vegan Chicken Burger

A crispy, juicy vegan chicken burger with fresh lettuce, tomato, red onion, and creamy vegan mayo. A delicious plant-based take on a classic fast-food favorite.

Ingredients

  • 2 vegan chicken-style burger patties
  • 2 burger buns
  • 2–3 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce: Mix the vegan mayonnaise, mustard, and lemon juice in a small bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Cook the patties: Heat the vegetable oil in a pan and cook the vegan chicken patties according to the package instructions until golden brown and crispy.
  3. Toast the buns: Cut the burger buns in half and lightly toast the cut sides in the pan.
  4. Build the burger: Spread the vegan mayo sauce on the bottom bun. Add lettuce, the crispy vegan chicken patty, tomato slices, and red onion.
  5. Spread a little more sauce on the top bun and place it on the burger.
  6. Serve: Serve immediately with crispy fries, a fresh salad, or your favorite vegan side dish.
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 10 minutes
Servings: 2

Tip: For an extra crispy burger, cook the patties in an air fryer or oven according to the package instructions. Add pickles or jalapeños if you like a little extra crunch and heat.
