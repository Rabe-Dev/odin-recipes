Easy Vegan Lasagna

A delicious, hearty vegan lasagna with rich tomato sauce, creamy béchamel, and layers of tender pasta. Comfort food at its best, without any animal products.

Ingredients

  • 9–12 lasagna sheets
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 1 zucchini, finely diced
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250 g vegan minced meat or plant-based mince
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 500 ml vegan béchamel sauce
  • 100 g vegan grated cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce: the olive oil in a large pan. Sauté the onion, garlic, carrot, and zucchini for 5–7 minutes.
  2. Add the vegan mince and cook for another 3–4 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste, chopped tomatoes, oregano, and basil. Season with salt and pepper and simmer for about 15 minutes.
  3. Layer the lasagna: Spread a little tomato sauce in a baking dish. Add a layer of lasagna sheets, followed by tomato sauce and béchamel. Repeat until all ingredients are used. Finish with a generous layer of béchamel and sprinkle with vegan cheese.
  4. Finish with a generous layer of béchamel and sprinkle with vegan cheese.
  5. Bake: Bake at 180°C / 350°F for approximately 35–40 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
  6. Let the lasagna rest for 5–10 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil if desired
Prep time: 20 minutes
Cooking time: 40 minutes
Servings: 4–6

Tip: Letting the lasagna rest before cutting helps the layers hold together.
