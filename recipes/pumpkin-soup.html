Creamy Vegan Pumpkin Soup

Easy Creamy Vegan Pumpkin Soup

A creamy, warming vegan pumpkin soup with roasted pumpkin, aromatic spices, and a hint of garlic. Simple, comforting, and perfect for a cozy autumn meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pumpkin, peeled and diced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 750 ml vegetable broth
  • 150 ml vegan cooking cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Pumpkin seeds for garnish
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Steps

  1. Prepare the vegetables: Heat the olive oil in a large pot. Sauté the onion and garlic for 2–3 minutes.
  2. Add the diced pumpkin, cumin, and nutmeg. Cook for another 3–4 minutes while stirring.
  3. Simmer: Pour in the vegetable broth and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for about 20 minutes, until the pumpkin is completely tender.
  4. Blend: Remove the pot from the heat and blend the soup until smooth and creamy.
  5. Stir in the vegan cooking cream and season with salt and black pepper.
  6. Serve: Pour the soup into bowls and garnish with a swirl of vegan cream, pumpkin seeds, and fresh parsley.
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cooking time: 25 minutes
Servings: 4

Tip: For extra flavor, roast the pumpkin in the oven before adding it to the soup. A little extra effort, because apparently pumpkins deserve a spa treatment before becoming dinner.
